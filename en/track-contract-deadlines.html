Keeping track of contract deadlines without the stress

Phone, electricity, insurance, gym: many contracts renew quietly when you miss the date. A simple overview and reminders keep your deadlines in view.

Step 1: Collect all contracts

For each contract note the provider, the fee, the end date, the notice period and where the contract is stored. Start with the contracts that cost money: mobile, internet, electricity and gas, insurance, streaming, gym, magazines.

Step 2: Enter the dates

Do not just enter the contract end; enter the cancel-by date. That is the date you really must not miss. How to work it out is covered in calculating a notice period.

Step 3: Get reminded in time

A reminder on the day of the deadline is too late. Allow one to two weeks of lead time so you can compare offers and write the letter. A spreadsheet, your phone calendar or dedicated software all work.

Step 4: Add up the costs

Add the fees per month and per year. This often shows which contracts you no longer need or should compare.

How Docloft helps

Docloft is a deadline and contract folder for Windows. You enter deadlines such as cancel-by, contract end, warranty, payment or expiry, one-off or recurring, and see what is overdue and what is due in the next 30 days at a glance. Windows notifications remind you with the lead time you choose, even when the app is closed (up to 200 reminder times within 400 days; open the app regularly so the schedule stays current). The overview shows what your contracts cost per month and per year and which you could cancel in the next 90 days. A calendar file (ICS) brings the deadlines to Outlook and your phone. A cancellation letter with contract number and date is ready in a minute to check, copy or email; only what you send yourself is sent. All data stays local on your PC, with no account and no cloud.

Note: the information comes from you. Docloft does not check statutory periods, contract terms or retention duties and offers no legal or tax advice.

Frequently asked questions

How often should I review my contracts?

A fixed date, for example once a quarter, is enough if every deadline also has its own reminder.

Does Docloft remind me when the app is closed?

Windows can show scheduled reminders even when the app is closed. Turn on Windows notifications for this. Up to 200 reminder times are scheduled within 400 days; open the app regularly to refresh the schedule.

Can I see the deadlines in my phone calendar?

Yes, Docloft can create a calendar file (ICS) that you add to Outlook or another calendar. You can keep the file in a folder such as OneDrive so your phone sees it too.
