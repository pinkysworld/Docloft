Scan documents and keep them searchable – offline on your PC

A scan alone is just a picture. Text recognition (OCR) is what makes contracts, invoices and letters searchable. It also works completely offline on your own PC.

Scanning: what helps

A flatbed scanner or your printer's scan function gives the best quality. Phone photos work too: line them up straight, light them well and avoid shadows. Straight, sharp originals are recognised better. Saving as PDF is usually best.

Understanding text recognition (OCR)

OCR turns the picture of a text into real text. After that you can search for words in the document. Recognition is never perfect: always check important details such as amounts, dates and contract numbers against the original.

Staying organised: naming and filing

  • Name files consistently, for example date_sender_type.
  • Sort by area of life (home, insurance, tax papers, contracts), not by month.
  • Keep originals that matter legally or practically in a safe place.
  • Make regular backups to a different drive.

Why offline?

Documents contain addresses, account numbers and contracts. If recognition and storage run locally, nothing leaves your computer. That suits anyone who does not want to create an account or upload documents.

How Docloft does it

In Docloft you drag in PDFs, scans and phone photos or a whole folder. The app checks the copy and detects duplicate files. Text recognition runs locally with Windows OCR and the Windows language packs you have installed; an installed Tesseract serves as a fallback. You review the suggestions for type, date and sender, add tags and decide what is saved. You search with word beginnings or without umlauts. Your original files stay unchanged where they are; the copies are ordinary files on your disk. A watched folder can pick up new phone scans automatically, and backups can be encrypted with a password.

Note: the app's text view is not a guaranteed reproduction of the original; check important details against the document itself.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an internet connection for text recognition?

Not with Docloft: recognition runs locally with Windows OCR. The Windows language packs for the document language must be installed.

What if text recognition finds nothing?

Check the Windows language pack for the document language under Settings > Time & language. Straight, well-lit scans help.

Are my original files changed?

No. Docloft works with checked copies and leaves the original file unchanged where it was.

This guide is general orientation. Docloft does not assess statutory retention duties or the evidential value of digital copies; ask a professional about those.
