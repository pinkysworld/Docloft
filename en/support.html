Support

In the app, Settings › “Report a problem …” opens a prepared e-mail with app version and error log that you check before sending. Or email mip@gmx.biz with your app version, Windows version and the steps leading to the problem. Remove personal details from screenshots and do not send documents or backups.

Text recognition returns no text

Check the Windows language pack for the document language in Settings › Time & language. Straight, well-lit scans help. An installed Tesseract is used as a fallback.

I receive no reminders

Windows can show scheduled reminders while the app is closed. Enable Windows notifications. Up to 200 reminder times are planned within 400 days; reopen the app regularly to update the schedule. Dates come from your input; please check them.

How do I back up my archive?

While in use, the app creates at most one database backup per day. It contains no document files; create a full ZIP backup and keep a copy on another drive.

How do I move to a new PC?

Create a full ZIP backup, copy it to the new PC, install Docloft and restore the backup.
