Frequently asked questions

What does Docloft cost?

One-time 24,99 € in the Microsoft Store, no subscription, updates for version 1 included. Refunds follow the Microsoft Store terms.

Are my originals preserved?

Yes. Docloft files a verified copy and leaves the original file unchanged in its existing location.

Where are my files stored?

Locally on your PC under %LOCALAPPDATA%\MinhStudios\Docloft. Document copies are ordinary files on your disk; there is no cloud sync.

How does text recognition work?

It runs locally with Windows OCR and installed Windows language packs. An installed Tesseract can be used as a fallback.

Do reminders appear when the app is closed?

Windows can show scheduled reminders while the app is closed. Enable Windows notifications. Up to 200 reminder times are planned within 400 days; reopen the app regularly to update the schedule. Dates come from your input; please check them.

Does Docloft check my notice periods?

No. The app stores your details and calculates using them. It does not check statutory deadlines, contract terms or retention duties and does not provide legal or tax advice.

Can I encrypt backups?

Yes, with a password (AES-256), including the weekly automatic backup. Without the password the backup cannot be opened – not even by us. Keep it somewhere safe.
